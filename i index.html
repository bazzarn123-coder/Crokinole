How to Aim & Flick:

Tap/Click and drag back from the active puck in your shooting rail to aim your shot. Release to flick! You can also slide your puck left/right on the outer rail before aiming.

Gameplay:

Players alternate turns taking 6 shots each. The player with the highest total score after all pucks are shot wins!
